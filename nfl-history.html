How these statistics were calculated
  • Each saved pick is compared with the winner recorded on its weekly tab. This is a reconstruction from the league files, not an independent audit of NFL game outcomes.
  • Correct and wrong picks are counted only for games with a recorded winning team. Blank winners—including ties or unscored games—do not count as losses.
  • Players with no picks that week are not assigned another player’s results. Missing individual picks are shown separately.
  • Rankings sort by correct picks; equal values share a rank. Percentage rankings require at least 200 scored picks in the selected scope.
  • Weekly leads require a fully recorded week and include shared leads. They are statistical leads, not a claim about payouts or league tiebreaker awards.
  • Regular-season leaders are highlighted only for 2023 and 2024, whose game-outcome coverage is complete.
  • Sources: NFL Football Picks - DRAFT (2022); NFL Football Picks 2023 - 2024; NFL Football Picks 2024 - 2025; NFL Football Picks 2025 - 2026. Season year means the fall in which the season began.
  • Playoffs and Super Bowl picks are kept out of these comparisons; the inspected Super Bowl sheets have no winner entered.
Why some totals differ from the old summary sheets

The old summary sheets contain copied standings and some counting differences. This page uses the actual weekly picks and recorded winners. It leaves your original spreadsheets unchanged.

  • 2023 BUMP’s summary combines Angie’s first seven weeks with Gary’s later weeks. Their personal records are separated here.
  • 2023 ISPY’s weekly picks total 126 correct; the season summary shows 125.
  • 2024 PUGTERA’s weekly picks total 124 correct; the season summary shows 123.
  • 2025 summary totals do not reconcile with the available weekly winners and show 276 decisions. No final 2025 champion is declared here.
