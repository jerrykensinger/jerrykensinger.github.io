How to play

The host is the judge and does not submit photos. Invite 2–12 players before starting. Every player submits one photo for the current letter. When all photos are in, the judge sees anonymous entries and picks a favorite—no formal criteria. The winner gets one point. Play A through Z for 26 total points. Highest score wins; ties share the title.

Use the same browser to return to your game. Test rooms expire after 30 days. The judge can delete a game and its photos. Only share the invite with people you want playing.
