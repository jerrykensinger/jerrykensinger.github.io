03 / Creative Development

Making stories feel like experiences.

A private interactive storytelling project combines original narratives, AI-assisted imagery, music and simple web games. The focus here is how those pieces fit together, rather than sharing the private experience itself.

CREATIVE WORKFLOW
Story → Sound → ExperienceConcept illustration · no audio or private assets

How music supports the narrative

Start with a story's mood and pacing. Draft lyrics around its theme, explore a suitable genre, and refine AI-generated music until it complements the scene. Instead of making the music the entire experience, use it as a payoff, transition or final reveal.

The same process applies to imagery and interactive elements: every piece should have a reason to be there.

01 / Design

Story first

Plan the narrative arc, interaction points and tone. Keep navigation clear and design for both desktop and mobile.

02 / Assemble

Match the media

Build the scenes, align visuals and music with the narrative, and test how the experience flows from one section to the next.

03 / Roll out

Make the reveal fun

Stage a release privately, test timing and interactions, use a teaser when appropriate, then open the experience to its intended audience.

This is a process-only case study. The private project, its access details, personal stories, images, lyrics and recordings are not published here.
