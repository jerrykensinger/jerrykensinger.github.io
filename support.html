Support the projects, not a paywall.

I build and share things here because I enjoy experimenting with technology, automation, and better ways to solve everyday problems. Everything on the site is free to browse.

Why this page exists

If something here helped you, support is welcome — never expected.

A useful idea, a troubleshooting write-up, or a project that saves someone time is the kind of thing I want this site to become. If you ever feel like helping me keep experimenting, sharing what I learn, or trying the next idea, you’ll be able to do that here.

No subscriptions. No locked posts. No guilt.

The site stays open. Support is simply an optional way to say, “That was useful — keep going.”
